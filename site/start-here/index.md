# Start here

Do these once, before sign-ups open on November 1.

1. [Install and update]({{ '/start-here/install/' | relative_url }}) the app on the coordinator's computer, and on any computer a letter cleaner or route planner will use.
2. [Create the season file]({{ '/start-here/season-file/' | relative_url }}) and put its passphrase in the club's password manager.
3. Take [a tour of the app]({{ '/start-here/tour/' | relative_url }}) using the demo season.
