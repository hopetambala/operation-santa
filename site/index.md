# Operation Santa guides

Every Christmas Eve, Pasadena Jaycees Santa teams visit hundreds of local families between 4:30 and 8:30 PM. The **Operation Santa** app is how the committee gets from the sign-up form to a printed packet in every team's car: one encrypted season file, routes that fit the evening, and letters and bag labels already sorted by route and stop.

These guides follow the season in order. Find the step you're on, or start with [your role]({{ '/roles/' | relative_url }}).

## What changed from 2025

In 2025 the committee did this with a sign-up export, a Python cleanup script, three MyRouteOnline accounts and a Word mail merge. It worked, but it was hard:

- **Routing started about 28 hours before delivery**, at noon on December 23, although home-visit sign-ups had closed about two weeks earlier.
- **Three people planned routes in three separate accounts**, so nobody could balance the whole city. Routes ran from 2 to 23 families.
- **A flat 10 minutes a stop** ignored apartments and big families. One route was planned past 8:30 PM and twelve more ran over three and a half hours.
- **The mail merge took about five tries.** "None" appeared in about 212 addresses, gate codes were on the letters but not on the route sheets, and about 160 children's drawings printed as raw web links.
- **Letters printed in sign-up order** and were sorted into 45 route bundles by hand.
- **Changes after printing** (12 stops dropped, 2 added, 1 moved) lived only in a spreadsheet, so the printed packets were out of date and nobody recorded why.

Each guide starts with a short **Why this step exists** note that ties the step back to one of these problems.

## How to read these guides

- **Bold text** is something on screen you click or choose, spelled exactly as the app shows it: **Accept import**, **Rebalance unlocked**.
- "Field" names are in quotes: "Season passphrase".
- ▸ means "then": Settings ▸ **Check for updates**.
- The examples use made-up families like the Rivera and Bennett families, the same fictional households the app's demo season shows. Never copy a real family's details into a guide, a chat or a screenshot.

{: .tip }
New to the app? Open it and explore the demo season first. Nothing you do there touches real data, and every guide works on it.
