# Glossary

**Avatar**
: The round button at the top right with your initial. Opens "Your season".

**Building**
: Every family at one street address, whatever their unit. A building always stays together on one route.

**Cleanup packet**
: A small encrypted file (`.opsanta-packet`) with some families' letters, sent to a volunteer letter cleaner. Not the same as a team packet.

**Coordinator holds**
: Letters kept back from the Santa teams, printed separately for the coordinator.

**Delivery**
: How a family gets their gifts. **home**: a Santa team visits. **pickup**: they come to the pickup event. **special**: handled outside the routes.

**Entry ID**
: The sign-up form's five-digit number for each family. Families added by hand start at 90001.

**Flag**
: A label on a family: spanish speaker, extra toys, adopt a family, do not share with volunteers, duplicate address ok, eaton impacted, paper letter, include out of area.

**Freeze**
: Locking the whole plan before printing. No rebalances, and changes need a reason.

**Geocoding**
: Turning an address into a point on the map.

**Lock (a route)**
: Keeping one route exactly as it is through rebalances and edits.

**Lock (the season)**
: Closing the season so it needs the passphrase again.

**Needs reprint**
: A printed route whose packet no longer matches the plan.

**North Pole**
: Where every route starts and ends on Christmas Eve.

**Pin (an address)**
: The point on the map where a family's front door is. **Pin is right** confirms it.

**Pin (to a route)**
: Keeping one family on its route through rebalances.

**Proposed flag**
: A flag the app suggests from the sign-up notes. Nothing changes until a person accepts it.

**Rebalance**
: Re-planning every unlocked ordinary route at once.

**Replacement pages**
: The pages of a team packet that changed, printed after a late change.

**Rudolph Route**
: The quiet name printed on letters for the form's poverty-level question.

**Service area**
: The cities the club visits, plus a buffer. Families outside it are **out of area**.

**Special route**
: A route that keeps its families through every rebalance, such as an apartment complex or an out-of-area run.

**Spread**
: The difference between the longest and shortest route. The goal is 45 minutes or less.

**Status**
: Where a family is in the season: new, verified, routed or cancelled.

**Target and hard maximum**
: How long a route should take, and the longest it may take and still print.

**Team packet**
: The printed set a Santa team takes on Christmas Eve: cover, route sheet, packing manifest, letters and labels.

**Version**
: The number on a printed packet. It goes up each time that route is printed.
