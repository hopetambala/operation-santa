# Brand

Operation Santa is the Pasadena Jaycees' planner for Christmas Eve gift deliveries: sign-ups in, routes planned, letters checked, team packets printed. Many of the people using it are older volunteers who use it a few times a year. Everything here serves one rule: **a first-time volunteer can read it, find it and press it without help.** Warmth comes from three holiday colors used sparingly, never from decoration.

Use this page when you make anything for Operation Santa: a screen in the app, a page in these guides, a sign-up flyer or a slide for the committee.

{: .note }
The mark is Operation Santa's own. It isn't the Pasadena Jaycees' logo and doesn't replace it. Anything that speaks for the chapter still uses the Jaycees' logo.

**The name is set in type, not drawn.** Put the mark first, then "Operation Santa" in Plus Jakarta Sans, extra bold, in ink. Under it, put "Pasadena Jaycees" smaller, in muted grey:

- Keep clear space around the mark of at least half its width.
- The smallest sizes are 16px for the mark alone and 32px for the mark with the name.
- Don't recolor the mark, rotate the star, add shadows or glows, or put it on a route color.
- Don't use it as a list bullet.

## Easy to read

- Text is at least 4.5 times darker or lighter than what's behind it, and control borders and the focus ring at least 3 times. That's the WCAG AA standard.
- **Nothing is smaller than 13px.** Body text is 16px and secondary lines are 14px.
- Every button, box and menu is at least 44px tall, so it's easy to hit.
- Steps and tabs always show their names. An icon may stand alone only for close (×), the up and down arrows, and pin.

## Type

**Plus Jakarta Sans** is used for everything, from 34px page titles down to 13px labels. Labels are uppercase and letter-spaced, as in table headings and read-outs like LATEST BACK. Big numbers such as times and counts use figures that line up. The font is free under the SIL Open Font License, and these guides serve their own copy of it.

A child's own Letter to Santa is set in **Georgia** on warm paper, so it reads as the child's words rather than the app's voice.

## Space, shape and depth

- Panels have rounded corners and one soft shadow. Don't put cards inside cards, and don't use gradients or colored side stripes.
- A screen reads top to bottom: the title, what's left to do, then the work.
- There is no decorative motion. The only animation is a spinner on an action that's running, and it stops for anyone whose computer is set to reduce motion.
- A disabled button is faded, and a nearby line says why, for example "Approve every letter first".

## Words

- **Plain words from the volunteer's side.** Say *family*, *route*, *stop*, *team*, *team lead*, *North Pole* (the place every route starts and ends), *letter*, *packet*, *sticker*. Never a system word like *geocode cache*, *entity* or *payload*, or a planning-document ID.
- **Say what happens.** A button names its action: "Plan routes", "Print all 45 routes (one PDF)", "Try saving again". The confirmation uses the same words: "45 routes planned. Longest 3h 41m."
- **A problem says what's wrong and what to do.** For example: "36 home deliveries have no route: 30 don't fit within the 3h 45m limit, 4 need an address, 2 are out of area. Route them, move them to pickup or cancel them in Route planner before printing every route." No apologies, no "Oops".
- **Count things.** Lead with what's left: "21 to check", "6 letters to look at", "1 needs a reprint".
- **Sentence case** for buttons, headings and tabs. Uppercase only for small labels.
- **Real formats:** "4:30 PM", "3h 45m", "Route 07", "#26014" for an Entry ID, "Unit 4B".
- **No emoji.** The print font can't draw them, and they read as decoration.
- Say **"you"** to the person using the app. The app never says "I" or "we".

## Icons

- Icons are [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) line icons: 18px in rows and buttons, 20px in the step bar, in the color of the text next to them.
- An icon always sits next to a word, except the four listed under [Easy to read](#easy-to-read).
- Holiday icons (tree, gift, star) appear only on empty screens and in the logo, never as markers in lists.

## What the brand doesn't cover yet

- **Printed packets** (route sheets, letters, stickers) keep their 2025 formats. Only the route lines on a packet's cover map use the route colors.
- **Dark mode:** the app is light only this season.
