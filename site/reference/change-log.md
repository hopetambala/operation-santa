# Change log
{: .no_toc }

**Who:** coordinator.

{: .why }
In 2025, changes made after printing were tracked in a spreadsheet with no reasons, so nobody could later say why a family had been dropped or moved. The app records every change as it happens.

Click your initial and choose **Change log**. The page lists every recorded change, newest first.

| Column | Shows |
|---|---|
| WHEN | Date and time. |
| WHO | The display name of whoever made it (Settings ▸ "Your display name"). |
| RECORD | The Entry ID, route, team, "Settings" or "Season" that changed. |
| CHANGE | What changed. Click it to see before and after. |
| REASON | The reason given for a change to a printed route, or "Before printing" if none was needed. |

What's recorded: imports and syncs, family edits and merges, address pins, flag decisions, letter reviews, merged cleanup packets, route moves, locks, pins, re-plans, special routes, team changes, freezing, and settings.

## Find a change

Each row's **Change** column says what changed, for example "route: Unrouted → Route 01". Long values are shortened; hover over one to see all of it.

Type in "Entry ID, route, field, reason…" to filter: an Entry ID like `26014`, a route like `Route 05` (it finds families moved onto or off that route), a word like `frozen`, a reason like `added late`, or a person's name. The page shows the newest 500 matches.

A family's own changes are also on their card's **History** tab in Families.

## Export

Click **Export change log (.csv)** to save `Operation Santa change log.csv` with every change: When, Who, Record, Field, Before, After and Reason.

{: .warning }
The export isn't encrypted, and the before and after values can include names and addresses. Keep it private and delete it after the season. The [purge]({{ '/after-the-season/purge/' | relative_url }}) clears the change log inside the app, but not exported copies.
