# November: sign-ups

The sign-up form opens around November 1 and home-visit sign-ups close in early December. You don't have to wait for the form to close: import as often as you like, and each import only adds and updates.

A good weekly rhythm for the coordinator:

1. [Import the Airtable CSV]({{ '/sign-ups/import-csv/' | relative_url }}) or [sync from Airtable]({{ '/sign-ups/airtable-sync/' | relative_url }}).
2. Work through [Address review]({{ '/sign-ups/address-review/' | relative_url }}): geocode, then duplicates, out of area and proposed flags.
3. Fix anything odd in [Families]({{ '/sign-ups/families/' | relative_url }}).
4. Send a batch of letters to a cleaner as a [cleanup packet]({{ '/sign-ups/cleanup-packets/' | relative_url }}), or [read them yourself]({{ '/sign-ups/letter-cleanup/' | relative_url }}).

The Overview's task list keeps count of what's left.
