# The North Pole on Christmas Eve
{: .no_toc }

**Who:** coordinator.
**When:** December 23 and 24.

1. TOC
{:toc}

{: .why }
Santa visits each family at one time on one evening. There's no second visit to plan and no live tracking from the field, so the app doesn't try to run the evening. Everything a team needs is on paper, and everything the coordinator needs fits on one table.

## The day before

- Make sure every route in the Print center says "Up to date". If any say "Needs reprint", print [replacement pages]({{ '/printing/reprints/' | relative_url }}) and call those leads.
- Text each team lead their **Phone links** (Print center ▸ the route's card ▸ **Phone links** ▸ **Copy**).
- Check that the North Pole phone number on the packets (Settings ▸ "North Pole phone (printed on packets)") will be answered all evening.

{: .note }
The app never offers an update from December 20 to December 26, so the copy you printed with is the copy you have on the day.

## Have these at the North Pole

- A spare printed copy of every team packet.
- The [Coordinator holds]({{ '/printing/pickup-event/' | relative_url }}#coordinator-holds) PDF, printed.
- The latest call list (Print center ▸ **Copy call list**), printed or on your phone, with every team lead's number.
- The laptop with the season, in case a family's details need checking. Lock it (**avatar** ▸ **Lock now**) when you step away.

## Changes on the day

A family calls on the afternoon of December 24 to cancel:

1. Open the season and set the family to **cancelled** with the reason **cancelled by family**.
2. Call the team lead and tell them to skip that stop.
3. There's no need to reprint: the app has recorded the change and why.

## Missed stops

When a team calls about a family who wasn't home, write it down on the spare packet. Gifts aren't left at the door, and there's no second visit.
