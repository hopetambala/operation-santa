# Santa teams
{: .no_toc }

**Who:** coordinator.
**When:** as volunteers sign up; every route needs a team before it can print.

1. TOC
{:toc}

{: .why }
A team lead's name and phone number go on the cover of their packet, and on the call list if their route changes after printing. The app also warns when a Spanish-speaking family is on a route whose team has no Spanish speaker. In 2025 team assignments lived in a spreadsheet next to the routes.

## Add a team

1. Go to **Teams** and click **Add team**.
2. Fill in the "Santa team" box:
   - "Team name" (required), for example "Team 7"
   - "Team lead"
   - "Lead phone"
   - "Notes"
   - tick "Spanish-speaking team member" if someone on the team speaks Spanish
3. Click **Save team**.

To change a team, click the **⋯** button on its card. **Remove team** is at the bottom of that box; it also takes the team off its route.

## Import teams from a spreadsheet

If the volunteer sign-up sheet is a spreadsheet, save it as CSV or Excel with these column headings. Use them exactly, in this order: the app matches headings by the words in them, so a heading like "Team lead" before "Team" can land in the wrong field.

| Team | Lead | Phone | Spanish | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Team 1 | Jamie | (626) 555-0100 | yes | Has a van |
| Team 2 | Casey | (626) 555-0101 | no | |

- "Spanish" counts as yes for `yes`, `y`, `sí`, `si`, `true` or `1`.
- A row whose team name matches an existing team updates that team. New names add new teams.
- Other columns, such as seats in the car, are ignored.

Click **Import CSV** and choose the file. The message says how many teams were imported.

## Give each route a team

1. Go to **Route planner** and click a route in the list.
2. Under the map, choose from **Team**. Each choice shows the lead's name, "ES" if the team has a Spanish speaker, and "(also Route 5)" if the team already has a route.
3. If the route has been printed, the app asks for a reason, and the route will need a reprint, because the team is on the cover.

A team can run two short routes: give both routes the same team. The Overview's task list counts "routes without a team" until every route has one.

Rebalancing keeps each route's team when the route keeps most of its families.
