**Who:** coordinator, print lead.
**When:** before the pickup event, and with the main print run.

1. TOC
{:toc}

## Pickup event packet

{: .note }
The committee is still deciding whether the pickup packet stays for 2026; it's part of the usability study. It works today.

{: .why }
In 2025, families who had relocated after the Eaton fire, or couldn't give a home address, came to a pickup event instead of a home visit: 44 families, plus 7 switched later. Their RSVPs lived in the free-text notes, and their letters and labels were a separate merge. The app keeps pickup families out of the routes and prints their packet from the same data.

Families are on the pickup list when their "Delivery" is **pickup**: set in [Families]({{ '/sign-ups/families/' | relative_url }}), by **Move to pickup** in [Address review]({{ '/sign-ups/address-review/' | relative_url }}), or from a proposed flag. Track each family's "Pickup RSVP" (waiting, confirmed, confirmed no kids) on their card.

To print:

1. Approve their letters in [Letter cleanup]({{ '/sign-ups/letter-cleanup/' | relative_url }}). The packet won't print while a pickup family's letter needs approval or an upload is still downloading.
2. Go to **Print center** ▸ "Other printouts and exports".
3. Click **Pickup event packet (N)**, where N is the number of pickup families. Save the PDF (`Pickup event - v1.pdf`).

The packet holds, sorted by family name:

1. A **check-in sheet**: Entry ID, family, RSVP, children, bags, and a line for a signature.
2. Each family's **letter**, in the same layout as the team packets.
3. **Bag labels**, titled "Pickup – #26014".

Print it again after changes; the version number goes up each time.

## Coordinator holds

Some letters shouldn't go out with the Santa teams: a single letter held with **Hold from print**, or a whole family flagged **do not share with volunteers**. The team packet only says "Letter held by coordinator. Ask the North Pole before delivering this family's gifts."

The coordinator keeps the full letters in a separate PDF:

1. Go to **Print center** ▸ "Other printouts and exports".
2. Click **Coordinator holds (N)**. Save the PDF (`Coordinator holds - 2026-12-19 1430.pdf`).

It has one letter page per held family, by Entry ID. Keep it with the coordinator at the North Pole.

{: .note }
For a family flagged **do not share with volunteers**, this PDF has their full letters. A single letter held with **Hold from print** still shows only "Letter held by coordinator." here too; read it on the **Held** tab in Letter cleanup.
