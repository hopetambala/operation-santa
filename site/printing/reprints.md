# Reprints and the call list
{: .no_toc }

**Who:** coordinator and print lead.
**When:** after any change to a route that has been printed, from December 19 to the morning of December 24.

1. TOC
{:toc}

{: .why }
In 2025 none of the changes made after printing (12 stops dropped, 2 added, 1 moved) reached the printed packets or the phone-app links. Teams had to be told separately, from a spreadsheet. Now the app knows what each packet looked like when it was printed, flags the packets that are out of date, says exactly what changed, and prints only the pages that need replacing.

## How a packet goes out of date

The app remembers what was on every page of each route's packet when it was printed. If anything that appears on paper changes afterwards, the route's card in the Print center switches from "Up to date" to **Needs reprint**. That includes:

- a family added, removed or moved to another route;
- the stop order;
- a family's name, address, gate code, phone, flags or printed notes;
- a child's details, letter or drawing;
- the team, lead or lead's phone, or the route's start time.

The Overview's task list shows "Reprint changed packets", and the Print center tab shows a count.

## See what changed

Each "Needs reprint" card lists the changes in plain words, for example:

> Added: #26088 Park F
> Removed: #26014 Bennett B
> Order changed for 3 stops
> #26031 Santos C: access changed

## Call the team

A "Call these teams" section lists each changed route with its team lead, their phone number, and what changed.

1. Click **Copy call list** to copy all of it.
2. Paste it into a message to whoever is phoning the leads, or into your own notes.
3. Call each lead and tell them what changed.

## Reprint

Choose one:

- **Replacement pages** (on the route's card or in the call list): a short PDF with the new cover, route sheet, packing manifest and labels, plus letters only for the families that were added or changed. Swap these into the team's packet.
- **Export PDF**: the whole packet again.
- **Needs reprint (N)** under "Check before printing": every out-of-date route's full packet in one PDF.

Replacement pages are stamped "replacement pages" and carry the new version number, so the team can see which pages are current. After printing, the card goes back to "Up to date".

{: .tip }
Tell the team lead to throw away the old route sheet, manifest and labels, and to keep the old letters for families who are still on the route.
