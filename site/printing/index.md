# December 19: printing

{: .why }
In 2025 the mail merge took about five attempts across different tools. Letters printed in sign-up order and were then sorted by hand into 45 route bundles, and the route sheets came from a different tool than the letters. Now one button makes a complete packet per team, already in stop order, and the app checks everything that would have gone wrong before it prints a page.

Order of work:

1. [Freeze the plan]({{ '/routing/late-changes/' | relative_url }}#freeze-the-plan).
2. Print an [alignment test page]({{ '/printing/labels/' | relative_url }}) on plain paper and check it against a label sheet.
3. [Check and print team packets]({{ '/printing/print-center/' | relative_url }}).
4. Print the [pickup event packet and coordinator holds]({{ '/printing/pickup-event/' | relative_url }}).
5. After any late change, print [replacement pages and call the team]({{ '/printing/reprints/' | relative_url }}).

If the app can't be used at all, there's a [MyRouteOnline fallback]({{ '/printing/myrouteonline-fallback/' | relative_url }}).

{: .warning }
Exported PDFs and CSVs are **not** encrypted. Save them to the print computer only, print them, and delete them after December 26. Every printed page says "Confidential family information. Shred after December 26."
