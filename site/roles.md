# Guides by role

A role is a job, not a login. One person can hold several, and the app doesn't ask who you are beyond a display name. Only the coordinator needs the season file and its passphrase.

## Coordinator

Owns the season file and its passphrase, imports sign-ups, decides on late changes, and purges the season afterwards.

5. [Families]({{ '/sign-ups/families/' | relative_url }})
6. [Address review]({{ '/sign-ups/address-review/' | relative_url }}): duplicates, out of area, proposed flags

## Route planner

Checks addresses, builds and balances the routes, and fits in late sign-ups. Works on the coordinator's computer or a copy of the season file the coordinator hands over.

1. [A tour of the app]({{ '/start-here/tour/' | relative_url }})
2. [Address review]({{ '/sign-ups/address-review/' | relative_url }}): geocoding and pins
3. [Route planner]({{ '/routing/route-planner/' | relative_url }})
4. [Late changes and the freeze]({{ '/routing/late-changes/' | relative_url }})
5. [MyRouteOnline fallback]({{ '/printing/myrouteonline-fallback/' | relative_url }}), only if the app can't be used

{: .warning }
Only one person can have a season file open at a time. If the planner and the coordinator share a file through a synced folder, agree on who has it open, and close the app when you're done.

## Letter cleaner

Looks at the letters the app flags (contact details, too long, empty, a bad upload) before they're printed; every other letter prints as imported. Works on the coordinator's computer, or from a **cleanup packet** (a small encrypted file with only some families' letters) when Settings ▸ Advanced ▸ "Send letters to helpers" is on.

1. [Install and update]({{ '/start-here/install/' | relative_url }})
2. [Cleanup packets]({{ '/sign-ups/cleanup-packets/' | relative_url }}): the "For letter cleaners" part
3. [Letter cleanup]({{ '/sign-ups/letter-cleanup/' | relative_url }})
